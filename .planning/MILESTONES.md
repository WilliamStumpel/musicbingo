# Project Milestones: Music Bingo

## v1.0 Paper Card System (Shipped: 2026-02-01)

**Delivered:** Complete application for running music bingo for a printed paper card game at venues.

**Phases completed:** 1-10 (33 plans total)

**Key accomplishments:**

- Local venue deployment with one-click startup and ngrok HTTPS tunneling
- Professional 4-up card printing with venue branding (up to 1000 cards)
- Manual playback mode - works with Spotify, Apple Music, or any player
- Host laptop controls + TV player display with delayed song reveal
- Winner tracking with card registration, proactive detection, and TV celebration
- Browser-based Game Prep UI with CSV upload and card generation
- Comprehensive testing with unit tests and Playwright E2E
